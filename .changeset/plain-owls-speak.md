The interface explains itself less and gets out of the way more. Every Settings page and section that carried a line of prose under its heading — "Agents work alongside your team on the same Issues", "Your Workspace's name, who can join it", the note about API keys being stored as a hash — now goes straight from the heading to the thing itself. The Inbox and Projects lost theirs too. What survives is the text that does work where it stands: the two lines introducing the commands that connect an Agent or an MCP client, the guidance in each empty state, and the dialogs that say what a choice commits you to. Those, and the rest of the app's copy, now speak to you rather than describing the system, and no environment variable name appears in the interface any more.
