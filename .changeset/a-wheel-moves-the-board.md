A wheel now moves the Board sideways. Every other screen in deevy reads downwards, so a mouse with one wheel could reach a Workflow's later States only by dragging the bar under the cards; turning the wheel over the columns now scrolls them. It stops at either end rather than swallowing the gesture, so the page still scrolls once the Board has nowhere left to go, and a trackpad's own sideways swipe — or Shift held down — is left exactly as it was. The bar itself is thinner, and drawn in the border colour rather than the platform's default shelf.
